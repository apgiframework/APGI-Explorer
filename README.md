# APGI-Signature

A psychological assessment app that helps users understand their cognitive and emotional processing styles based on the Active Predictive Guidance Integration (APGI) model.

## Features

- **Interactive Quiz**: Complete a series of questions to assess your psychological profile
- **Detailed Profile Analysis**: Receive a personalized APGI profile based on your responses
- **Multiple Profile Types**: Discover which of the 8 APGI profiles best matches your cognitive style
- **Psychological States Explorer**: Browse and learn about different psychological states and how they relate to your profile
- **Save & Export**: Save your results for future reference and export them for sharing or professional consultation

## Profile Types

The app identifies your cognitive style across several dimensions:

- **Prediction Error Sensitivity (ε)**: How attuned you are to unexpected events
- **Precision Allocation (π)**: How you allocate attention to sensory input
- **Ignition Threshold (θₜ)**: Your threshold for taking action
- **Somatic Bias (β)**: How much you rely on bodily sensations

Based on these dimensions, you'll be matched with one of 8 profile types:

1. **Sensitive Integrator**: High awareness of prediction errors and bodily responses
2. **Analytical Guard**: Strong mental models with precise vigilance
3. **Adaptive Balancer**: Balances between exploration and stability
4. **Open Explorer**: Thrives on novelty and new experiences
5. **Contemplative Anchor**: Maintains calm and reflection in various contexts
6. **Somatic Navigator**: Deeply connected to bodily sensations
7. **Cognitive Architect**: Strong theoretical and planning abilities
8. **Dynamic Responder**: Quick to respond to emotional situations

## Getting Started

1. Launch the app and select "Start Quiz"
2. Answer the questions honestly based on your experiences
3. View your detailed profile analysis
4. Explore your psychological states and strategies
5. Save your results for future reference
